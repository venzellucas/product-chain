# Research format → `02-research.md`

Goal: find out whether this already exists, whether people want it, and where the opening is (if any).
Be evidence-led — cite sources (URLs) for every claim. Distinguish facts from inference.

```markdown
# Research: <slug>

## Incumbents & alternatives
For each meaningful competitor or substitute (including "they just use a spreadsheet / do nothing"):
- **<name>** — what it does, who it's for, pricing, strengths, gaps. [source]

## Demand signals
Evidence people actually want this: search volume, communities/threads complaining, existing tools'
traction, willingness to pay, hiring/spend trends. Note where signals are weak or absent. [sources]

## Market
Rough size and direction (growing / flat / shrinking) and why. Order-of-magnitude is fine; show the
basis. [sources]

## Differentiation gap
Where is the unmet need the incumbents miss — or is the space saturated with no real opening? Be
honest; "no clear gap" is a valid and valuable finding.

## Synthesis
3–5 bullets a decision-maker needs: is the pain real, is it crowded, where's the wedge, what's unknown.
```

A single researcher covers one angle/competitor in depth. The orchestrator merges several into the
final doc above.
